## Reflection

**What worked:** The theremin was a very expressive instrument because the performers actions directly impact the sound they produce. It was a huge breakthrough for creating a relationship between gesture and sound.

**What didn't:** One of the biggest issues with the theremin was that it was extremely difficult to play. With no physical feedback, it was nearly impossible to know where the correct notes were. The smallest movement could create large changes in pitch, requiring a lot of precision.
