## Results

- The group brainstorm at the time felt unproductive. We came up with some new ideas, but there was not one that unified us. However, filling out the instrument worksheet with the spinning rope idea fixed that!
- We came up with some priorities for our instrument:
  - Talal: High degree of expression in the music. (Not a drone sound)
  - Caleb: Instrument should take multiple types of input - continuous and discrete.
  - Sam: Creative and relevant use of ML.
  
- I came up with the idea of a spinning whip and gloves instrument. It would map a whip motion to a fast attack and steadier motions to legato. It would consider the speed of rotation as a synthesis control. It would interact musically with the air column created through fast rotation. The gloves would provide discrete input for note on signals. The ML would handle the parameter mapping and facilitate switching hands in performance.
  - The inspiration came from the fun I used to have as a kid spinning beaded necklaces around.
