## Methods

I started with my own collection of wearable technologies on social media apps such as instagram, and came across small and simple wearable tech. While they were visually pleasing, they were not exactly fitting the ML or Instrument categories.

Additionally, I came across this website for Gesture Synth (https://gesture-synth.com/): This is a webcam based hand gesture software, utilizing a device's camera to read inputs from hand gestures. Although, it is possible to use without a camera and just with mouse input.

Another hand-gesture related source I found was a paper called  Exploiting domain transformation and deep learning for hand gesture recognition using a low-cost dataglove (https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-022-25108-2), which I was initially attracted to because it mentioned a low-cost dataglove. While I do try to avoid hand-gesture related sources, I thought this one was useful to look at to get a general grasp of the general Machine Learning functionality behind gestural interfaces.
