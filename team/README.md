## Required reading

**Rebecca Fiebrink and Laetitia Sonami**, *Reflections on Eight Years of
Instrument Creation with Machine Learning*. NIME 2020, Birmingham, pp. 237–242.

- Paper: https://www.nime.org/proceedings/2020/nime2020_paper45.pdf
- The whole NIME archive, which is open access and searchable:
  https://www.nime.org/archives/

Six pages. No technical background needed.
